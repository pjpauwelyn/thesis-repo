CENTRALITY SCALE
────────────────────────────
Assign centrality based on how much the answer changes if this concept is missing.

  1.0          CRITICAL     — answer is impossible without this concept
  0.7 – 0.9   IMPORTANT    — significantly shapes or constrains the answer

Multiple pairs may share 1.0 for complex multi-mechanism questions.
Do NOT cluster everything at 0.7–0.9 out of uncertainty — use 1.0 or 0.4–0.6 when warranted.
